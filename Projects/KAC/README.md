## Source handling
- 1,478 local files indexed; 179 Drive items discovered, including 75 folders and 104 files. Three temporary Office lock files are excluded from overlap comparison.
- 19 selected local documents extracted into Markdown and three Drive text supplements retained. Raw source datasets are linked and indexed rather than duplicated.
- Five exact local duplicate groups were identified by SHA-256. 72 Drive files have normalized-name matches; 29 have no local name match. These are candidate relationships, not confirmed cross-source identity.
- Drive-native documents, PDF renditions, renamed files and snapshots stay separate until reviewed. Nothing is deleted, overwritten or automatically declared newest.
- The user's personal-email access arrangement is recorded as user-provided context. No personal account was accessed, no permissions were changed, and no new synchronization or export was established.
- Missing access and discovery limits are documented in Sources/. Contents are a first ingestion pass, not a complete verification of every dataset and version.
