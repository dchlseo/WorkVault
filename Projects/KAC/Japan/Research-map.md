## Classification found in strategy notes
A direct access; B price barriers; C multi-airport/regional gateways; D search/booking/payment/language/luggage friction; E conversion into tourism/stays/spending.
These categories can overlap: do not count the same case multiple times as independent evidence.

## Verification requirements
Distinguish program existence, operating use, budget authorization, actual spending, continued operation, additional effects and causal evidence. A budget ceiling is not expenditure.
Preserve original Japanese titles and original URLs; confirm applicable year and operator before moving a case into a client-facing conclusion.
