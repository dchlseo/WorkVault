## Findings as reported, not recalculated here
The comparative source links rental-car use with broader regional travel. This is an association and may reflect self-selection.
Korean survey-based estimated use is a person-level proxy; Japanese movement-mode share uses trip segments. Do not treat their percentage difference as a policy-performance gap.
A platform search returning zero products does not establish absence of operators or vehicles.

## Proposed service design
Diagnose airport → city/interchange and onward tourism-region movement separately.
Compare rail/bus, tourism shuttle, taxi/DRT and rental car by traveler type, destination, arrival time, language/payment, luggage and reservation requirements.
Rental car is one option, including one-way return where feasible; no universal mode is selected.

## Survey fields proposed in the preparation
Official timetable and season/day; first/last departure; wait/transfer/walk; total fare and journey time; booking/payment/language; vehicle pickup/return/insurance; operating authority, funding and jurisdiction; source URL, retrieval/effective date and unresolved questions.
Coverage of seven airports and detailed Daegu/Gimhae work are proposed scope, not confirmed assignments or a completed survey.
