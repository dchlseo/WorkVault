## Proposals found
- Assess overseas demand and regional tourism fit, then rights/permissions, both airports' feasible times, facilities, carrier network and economics.
- Consider expansion of existing markets and new connections as separate paths.
- Use Daegu–Taiwan as an illustrative validation case and Gimhae–Japan as a comparison; not an approved shortlist.
- Separate additional inbound demand from substitution between airlines or Korean airports.

## Evidence caution
KAC 2025 departures do not directly identify inbound itinerary/entry carrier. High foreign-passenger demand and low foreign-carrier share do not prove profitable entry or carrier willingness.
Airportal data include general passengers, not nationality by themselves. Combining different years or planned flights and realized passengers does not produce a valid load factor.
The November 6 preparation flags inconsistent carrier/denominator text in draft materials. Check original tables and current operating facts before reuse.

Rights/incentive source files remain in the [area register](README.md). A historical schedule or incentive workbook does not establish current legal permissions, available slots, funding or approval.
